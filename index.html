03 / HOW I APPROACH DESIGN

Thoughtful by
process.

A flexible approach that keeps the problem, the people using the product, and the details of the interface in view.

01FOUNDATION

Understand.

Clarify the goal, identify the user’s task and organise the information that matters.

START WITH WHY↗
02DIRECTION

Explore.

Map the journey, compare directions and create wireframes before polishing the details.

MAKE OPTIONS↗
03CRAFT

Design.

Build hierarchy, typography, components and interactions into a consistent interface.

BRING IT TO LIFE↗
04DETAIL

Refine.

Review the details, check different screen sizes and improve the experience where needed.

MAKE IT CLEARER↗
✳

One thoughtful loop, not a rigid checklist. Design is iterative.

I’m Nisarg, an MCA student from Ahmedabad, India, building my foundation in UI/UX design.

I enjoy the space where technology meets people: understanding a task, organising information and shaping interfaces that feel clear and approachable. I’m learning by working through projects, paying attention to the small details and getting better at explaining the reason behind each design decision.
